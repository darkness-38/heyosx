<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Implement Master/Stack geometry logic</name>
  <files>heydm/src/window.rs</files>
  <action>
    - Fill in `recompute_layout` for `Layout::Tiling`.
    - Algorithm:
      - Define useable area: `output_size` minus `panel_height` at top.
      - If no windows, return.
      - If windows <= `master_count`, divide useable area vertically among all windows.
      - Else:
        - Split area into Master (left) and Stack (right) using `master_ratio`.
        - Divide Master area vertically among `master_count` windows.
        - Divide Stack area vertically among remaining windows.
    - Exclude fullscreen windows from tiling (they keep their fullscreen geometry).
  </action>
  <verify>
    <automated>cargo check -p heydm</automated>
  </verify>
  <done>Master/Stack geometry correctly calculated for all managed windows.</done>
</task>
